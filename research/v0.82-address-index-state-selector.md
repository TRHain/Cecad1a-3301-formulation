# v0.82 — Protocol numbers as address, index, and state selectors

## Decision

**NO_ADDRESS_OR_STATE_SELECTOR_EVIDENCE**

No frozen discovery cell passed both family-corrected controls, so no candidate
advanced. The unchanged held-out run also produced no dual-control cell and no
replication. The separately frozen Page 36 corroborative panel produced no
dual-control hit. Red-event state remained **OFF**, and no plaintext or language
score was generated.

The v0.81 result remains a hard negative: 761, 1033, and 3301 are rejected as
literal long carrier periods under the frozen projection family. v0.82 did not
retest that hypothesis or change its metrics.

## Frozen family

The tested source values were `29, 58, 133, 167, 331, 761, 1033, 3301`.
Before scoring, the following choices were frozen in
`analysis/v0.82-protocol.json`:

- transforms: identity, prime rank, nth prime, Page 32 Fibonacci ordinal, and
  the prime at that Page 32 ordinal;
- one-based bounded and wrapped addressing, each forward and reverse;
- split-full, per-section, and per-page target units;
- address scores: strong-boundary hits, selector-state matches, rune
  collisions, and exact reversal-pair concordance;
- state scores using the seven unique selector residues modulo 29: strong
  boundary rate, next-rune concentration, and adjacent-equality rate;
- 20,000 structured-number controls and 20,000 within-unit circular-shift
  controls (plus matched residue controls for state tests);
- single-step standardized maxT correction across the complete frozen family;
- an exact discovery cell had to have FWER `p <= 0.05` under both relevant
  controls, then replicate held out under the unchanged rule and direction.

This covers split-local full streams, every section stream, every page-local
stream, prime ordinal/value transforms, and Page 32 Fibonacci-derived ordinal
and prime indices. Absolute positions in the unsplit full canonical stream are
also emitted rune-by-rune in `absolute_global_landings_descriptive`; they are
descriptive only because scoring them across the discovery boundary would
invalidate the frozen split.

The corpus and split are unchanged from v0.81: canonical SHA-256
`5e0003aa7f7dde3b238d17c5f964790dbf6bf9db5edacc4a5ec0bad81c6f4bce`,
6,057 discovery runes in sections 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.9, and 0.10, and 6,808
held-out runes in sections 0.8, 0.11, and 0.12.

## Corpus results

The discovery family contained 240 address cells and 9 state cells. It had no
survivors. Its smallest structured-number address FWER value was `0.03290` for
per-page, identity, bounded, reverse, reversal-pair concordance, but the same
cell had within-unit FWER `1.0`. The smallest within-unit address FWER anywhere
in the family was `0.56207`. The smallest state residue-control FWER was
`0.28429`; no state cell survived both controls.

Held out, the smallest structured-number address FWER was `0.00915` for a
different rule (per-page, identity, bounded, forward, reversal-pair
concordance), but its within-unit FWER was `1.0`. The smallest within-unit
address FWER was `0.58427`. The smallest state residue-control FWER was
`0.25944`, and all held-out state within-unit FWER values were `1.0`. These are
control-specific negative findings, not leads. There were zero discovery
survivors and zero held-out replications.

## Page 36 geometry audit and 91-glyph artifact

The original 2400×3600 image is checked in byte-for-byte as
`liber-primus/original-onion7/36.jpg` (SHA-256
`3b54a31a9e796e2c01b4e80d46e1092dee2e1142a7c61873e282078e2c776cb9`).
It shows five contiguous ordinary horizontal rune rows below the two sigil
rows, with rune counts `23 + 24 + 22 + 22 + 8 = 99`. The fifth row is followed
by a red dotted mark and later a red section numeral. There is no visible
boundary after the first four rows.

Therefore, 91 is not intrinsic to the image geometry. It is the frozen
community-transcription slice at canonical lines 453–456; line 457 is the
contiguous fifth row. `91 = 7×13` remains an observation only, with no
independent structural support and no grid, spiral, or score-selected reading
order tested.

The exact 91-rune slice is preserved in
`liber-primus/page36-91-artifact.json`, including row structure, rune indices
and Gematria primes, visible word marks, token bounding boxes and centroids,
transcription-only line terminators, six predeclared reading-order
alternatives, and its relationship to the preceding sigils and following
eight-rune row. The artifact SHA-256 is
`3e49e670ddb2c3ef34fef2c4e276620b23bad05636545bd20654f7c13dcb32ec`.
This preservation does not assume that the 91 runes are ciphertext.

The Page 36 panel was frozen only after corpus discovery had closed with zero
survivors. Across 648 cells and 20,000 resamples per control, it produced zero
dual-control descriptive hits and the decision
`NO_PAGE36_ADDRESS_ANOMALY`. Its smallest structured-number FWER was `0.01955`,
but the same cell did not pass the circular-order control. The smallest
circular-order FWER anywhere was `0.17424`.
