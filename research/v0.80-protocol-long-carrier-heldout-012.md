# v0.80 — Protocol-derived long-carrier screen on held-out LP2 section 0.12

## Goal
Test the frozen protocol/source selector family **without using red events** on a longer independent LP2 region. This follows the v0.63/v0.67 section-0.11 frequency work and the onion/protocol architecture result in v0.79.

## Target
LP2 section 0.12, pages 40–55. Canonical section metadata places this section across those pages. Rune extraction yielded **3316 rune glyphs**. The page-49–51 base-60 object was not converted into runes; only actual rune glyphs were included.

## Frozen selector family
`29, 58, 133, 167, 331, 761, 1033, 3301`

These were fixed before the test from the rune alphabet/page count and preserved Cicada source/protocol artifacts. They were not optimized after viewing the target.

## Signals
Three ciphertext-only signals were tested:

1. rune index, mean-centered;
2. adjacent equality indicator;
3. signed modular ciphertext delta in Z_29.

For every selector P, the statistic was the normalized squared complex projection at frequency 1/P. For each signal, **5000 marginal-preserving permutations** were used and the null statistic was the maximum over all eight frozen selectors, giving family correction across the selector set.

## Results
Family-corrected empirical p-values:

- rune-centered: **p = 0.8640**
- equality: **p = 0.8456**
- ciphertext delta: **p = 0.2488**

Top raw periods:

- rune-centered: **761**, then 133, 58, 1033
- equality: 29, 133, 3301, 58
- delta: 58, **761**, 29, 133

The repeated appearance of 761 is descriptive only. It does not survive correction or independent held-out validation.

## Decision
**NO_PROTOCOL_LONG_CARRIER_EVIDENCE_IN_HELDOUT_012**

This rejects a simple protocol-number oscillator/carrier fingerprint on section 0.12. It does **not** reject protocol-derived selectors as initialization/state inputs to a non-periodic prime/stateful carrier.

Red was deliberately excluded. The next permitted model is therefore a frozen protocol selector controlling a prime-derived/stateful carrier, with independent held-out validation before red is introduced.
