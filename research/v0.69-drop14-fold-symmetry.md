# v0.69 — 14-drop count and fold/mirror test

## What is established

The archived 2012 `845145127` coordinate page contains exactly **14 coordinate rows**. A later community reconstruction notes that one OutGuess copy preserved only 12, with Fayetteville and one Seoul coordinate absent there; physical retrieval reports support those two additional site coordinates. Therefore 14 is not merely a modern reconstruction artifact.

## Why 14 is interesting, but not yet evidence

- 14 = 2 × 7, so it naturally supports seven folded pairs.
- 14 is not prime and is not itself Fibonacci.
- The later Liber Primus Page-32 prime-ordinal spiral contains **14 as its seventh entry**: `[1,2,3,4,6,9,14,...]`.
- Because the coordinate stage predates Liber Primus, the 14↔Page32 connection is descriptive and potentially interesting, but not independent proof.

## Ordered geographic fold test

The published coordinate order was folded around its midpoint: row 1↔14, 2↔13, ..., 7↔8. A combined latitude/equatorial plus longitude/prime-meridian mirror score was compared with 200,000 random reorderings.

- Combined fold empirical p ≈ **0.9887**
- Latitude-only fold empirical p ≈ **0.7654**

Decision: **NO ORDERED GEOGRAPHIC FOLD EVIDENCE**. The published GPS list itself does not appear to be a simple bilateral geographic mirror.

## Cicada image symmetry

The archived `cicada.jpg` displayed on the 2012 coordinate site is visibly approximately bilateral about the insect body axis, but the wing venation and body details are not exact mirror copies. This is compatible with a deliberate symmetry motif, but exact asymmetry must be measured on the original archived JPEG bytes before treating the residual as encoded data.

## Next test

Treat folding as a transformation family rather than assuming the coordinates themselves mirror geographically:

1. measure image left/right residual after centerline reflection;
2. test whether residual pixels cluster around QR/wing/body landmarks;
3. test page/rune streams under center-fold permutations and reversed-half operators;
4. keep these tests independent from the frequency/state search until a controlled result survives matched nulls.
