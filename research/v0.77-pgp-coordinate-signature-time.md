# v0.77 — PGP timestamp of the signed 12-coordinate payload

## Exact packet metadata
Parsing the detached signature packet from the preserved `2012-01-coordinates.asc` artifact gives:

- OpenPGP signature version: 4
- RSA algorithm: 1
- Hash algorithm: SHA-1 / digest algo 2
- Issuer key ID: `181F01E57A35090F`
- Signature creation epoch: `1326062075`
- Signature creation UTC: **2012-01-08 22:34:35 UTC**

The coordinate webpage/countdown reveal is documented at **2012-01-09 17:00:00 UTC**.

Therefore the signed 12-coordinate artifact was created **66,325 seconds = 18 h 25 m 25 s before the public reveal**.

## Connection to v0.76
v0.76 established that both the signed 12-row list and webpage 14-row list are strictly ordered by descending latitude. The two webpage-only rows (Seoul A and Fayetteville) insert exactly into that ordering to reconstruct the 14-row webpage list.

The signature timestamp now provides a concrete operational explanation: the PGP-signed image payload was pre-generated at least ~18.4 hours before reveal, while the webpage could have been updated later with two additional drop locations without regenerating/re-signing/re-steganographing the JPEG.

This is not proof of the deployment workflow, but it is substantially more parsimonious than treating 12+2 as key material.

## Decision
`12_PLUS_2_VERSIONING_EXPLANATION_STRENGTHENED`

Deprioritize 12+2 cryptographic-key theories unless independent evidence contradicts this timing/order explanation.
