# v0.71 — Page-centered fold residual test

We followed the center-fold / inkblot hypothesis into the ciphertext without changing the main carrier/state track.

## Test

Section 0.11 was split at the previously established page boundaries:

`[0,123,384,655,893,1121,1349,1589]`

Each page segment was folded about its own midpoint. For each mirrored pair we computed the rune residual `(left-right) mod 29` and measured:

- residual entropy,
- top-3 residue concentration,
- exact-zero residual rate,
- a preregistered replication of v0.70's post-hoc 58-pair near-center enrichment.

Null controls independently shuffled runes inside each page, preserving page lengths and page-level marginals, and reran the exact same statistics over 10,000 trials.

## Results

- mean residual entropy: **4.6733 bits**, low-tail p = **0.8344**
- mean top-3 residual mass: **0.1980**, high-tail p = **0.6040**
- mean zero residual rate: **0.02305**, high-tail p = **0.9615**
- frozen center-58 replication: **9 / 406 = 2.22%**, p = **0.9397**

Decision: **NO_PAGE_FOLD_RESIDUAL_EVIDENCE**.

The 58-pair center enrichment from v0.70 failed decisively when frozen and transferred to page-centered folds. This removes simple section-midpoint and page-midpoint mirror/residual mechanisms from the active shortlist.

## Interpretation

The approximate bilateral symmetry of the cicada artwork can still be deliberate visual language, but a literal fold of section/page rune order does not reproduce a detectable cipher structure under these tests. The artwork-difference-image hypothesis remains separate and requires original-byte image provenance before pixel asymmetries are treated as data.
