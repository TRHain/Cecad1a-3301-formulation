# v0.73 — Exhaustive 12+2 Leave-Two-Out Test

## Question
A preserved 2012 artifact contains 12 coordinates while the authenticated coordinate page presents 14. The historically absent pair is Seoul B + Fayetteville. Test whether those two are exceptional against every possible 2-coordinate omission from the 14.

## Method
All C(14,2) = 91 omission pairs were evaluated under the same frozen metrics:

- maximum Rayleigh concentration over the predeclared harmonic family: 13, 17, 29, 133, 167, 331, 761, 1033, 3301;
- circular-longitude gap uniformity;
- mean nearest-neighbor geographic dispersion;
- pairwise angular distance of the omitted pair itself.

No metric was chosen after seeing the historical pair.

## Historical omission
- Seoul B
- Fayetteville

The resulting 12-location subset had:

- max Rayleigh R = 0.6402757410 at period 133;
- longitude-gap CV = 1.0939593132;
- mean nearest-neighbor angular distance = 0.3228779703 rad.

## Exact rank among 91 possible omitted pairs

- Rayleigh concentration: **11 / 91**
- Longitude-gap uniformity: **32 / 91**
- Geographic dispersion: **13 / 91**
- Omitted-pair closeness: **73 / 91**
- Omitted-pair farness: **19 / 91**

## Decision
**NO_12_PLUS_2_EXCEPTIONALITY** under these geometric/frequency metrics.

The historical Seoul B + Fayetteville omission is mildly high-ranked for Rayleigh concentration and geographic dispersion, but nowhere near the predeclared significance threshold. The 12+2 split remains historically interesting, but there is no evidence yet that the two extra coordinates function as a geometric/frequency verification pair.

## Interpretation
Do not label the two coordinates fake. Preserve the stronger formulation: 12-coordinate artifact vs 14-coordinate authenticated page may reflect publication/version/provenance differences or a non-geometric role. Future tests should target authentication/order/checksum-like relationships rather than reusing geographic structure metrics.
