# v0.81 — Frozen protocol selector × long-carrier experiment

## Decision

**NO_PROTOCOL_SELECTOR_LONG_CARRIER_EVIDENCE**

No model met the frozen requirement to improve on discovery and held-out data
under both matched controls. Red-event state remained **OFF** throughout. No
plaintext was generated or scored, and no threshold, split, selector, period,
or signal was changed after the run.

## Frozen family

- Long periods: 761, 1033, 3301
- Signals: mean-centered rune index, adjacent equality, signed modular delta in
  Z_29
- Metric: normalized squared complex projection at frequency `1/P`
- Family statistic: maximum over the three long periods
- Matched null: 5,000 independent within-section signal permutations
- Period control: 5,000 three-period families sampled without replacement from
  integer periods 761 through 3301
- Empirical p-values use `(exceedances + 1) / 5001`
- Promotion alpha: 0.05 on discovery and held out under both controls, with the
  same top period

The eight frozen protocol numbers were evaluated as constant phase selectors.
For a squared complex projection, adding a constant phase only multiplies the
projection by a unit complex number and cannot change its power. The numerical
audit's largest difference was `2.51e-18`. The selector cross therefore
collapses algebraically rather than constituting 24 distinct models. Treating
the duplicate phases as independent trials would be false multiplicity.

## Results

### Discovery

| Signal | Top period | Family max | Permutation p | Random-period p |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rune-centered | 1033 | 0.000278831 | 0.3309 | 0.0746 |
| Equality | 3301 | 0.000251566 | 0.8134 | 0.6267 |
| Signed delta | 1033 | 0.000646144 | 0.0358 | 0.2362 |

The signed-delta discovery result passed only the fixed-period permutation
control. It did not outperform arbitrary matched long periods and therefore
was not eligible for promotion even before held-out evaluation.

### Held out

| Signal | Top period | Family max | Permutation p | Random-period p |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rune-centered | 761 | 0.000257023 | 0.3857 | 0.2324 |
| Equality | 3301 | 0.000670410 | 0.1320 | 0.0018 |
| Signed delta | 3301 | 0.000211609 | 0.5821 | 0.2597 |

The held-out equality result was unusual only relative to randomly selected
periods. It failed the matched permutation control and had no discovery
support. The discovery signed-delta top period changed from 1033 to 3301 held
out and its permutation p-value degraded to 0.5821.

## Interpretation after controls and validation

The frozen periods do not show a reproducible direct long-carrier projection
in these ciphertext-only signals. The isolated control-specific near-misses are
recorded as negative findings and must not be used to tune a follow-up family.
Because nothing was promoted, the predeclared red-independent replication was
not triggered and red-event state remains sealed for this experiment.
