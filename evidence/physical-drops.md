# Physical Drop Evidence

## Purpose

Preserve the physical-location evidence as a separate research track and test it without feeding post-hoc geographic coincidences into the cipher search.

The archived 2012 coordinates comprise 14 locations across Warsaw, Paris, Seattle, Seoul, Fayetteville, Chino, New Orleans, Miami, Haleiwa and Erskineville. Contemporary archive material describes physical posters bearing Cicada imagery and QR codes at the coordinates.

## Objective measurements to collect

For each surviving poster/drop photograph:

- highest-quality surviving file and provenance;
- file hash, dimensions and available metadata;
- artifact type: original puzzle image, photograph of a physical artifact, or later reproduction;
- poster orientation and QR orientation;
- cicada-symbol orientation;
- mounting surface / fixture type;
- objectively measurable geometry in the image;
- coordinate, longitude-derived phase, latitude, UTC offset and local solar-time quantities;
- discovery chronology where independently documented;
- electrical-grid frequency only as a predeclared 50/60-Hz environmental variable, never as a retrofitted explanation.

## Frequency / phase hypothesis

Test whether physical locations behave as phase or periodic variables rather than merely map destinations. Predeclared quantities include longitude as phase on a 360-degree cycle, great-circle bearing/distance, UTC offset, 50/60-Hz grid class, and authenticated Cicada number families (3301/1033/331/133/167/761, primes, Fibonacci).

Any association must survive multiple-testing correction and should replicate across 2012 vs 2013 rather than being fitted jointly.

## Acquisition rule

Do not analyze JPEG noise, compression residue or EXIF as a Cicada signal until the provenance of that exact file is known. Camera artifacts in photographs of posters are not equivalent to steganography in Cicada-distributed source images.
